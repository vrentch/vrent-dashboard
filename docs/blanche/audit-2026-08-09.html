Blanche Beauty Web & systems audit 9 August 2026 Read-only — nothing was changed

The live site is the shopfront. The business is in the back room.

Blanche runs two Wix sites. The one customers reach — the premium one on the real domain — has no booking, no address, no phone and the wrong time zone. The one nobody is looking at holds all 302 customer records and all 39 treatments.

Headline finding

A customer cannot book an appointment on blanchebeautyuk.com. There are zero bookable services on it.

Every treatment Blanche sells — all 39, with prices, durations and deposits — is configured on the old free wixsite.com address instead. So is the customer list: 302 contacts there against 10 on the live site.

What is broken

Ordered by what costs money soonest, not by how hard it is to fix.

Critical
No way to book on the live site

Wix Bookings is not installed on the live site and it has no services. A customer who finds Blanche through the real domain has no path to an appointment at all — they must find a phone number, which is also missing.

POST /bookings/v2/services/count → {"count": 0}
Critical
302 customer records are stranded on the free site

The real customer list, with treatment history labels like gel-polish-off-on, sits on the old wixsite.com property. The live site has 10 contacts, one of which is the owner's own account. Any plan that retires the old site without migrating first destroys the customer database.

live: total 10 · old: total 302 (GET /contacts/v4/contacts)
High
Live site time zone is set to Europe/Kyiv

A UK salon on a Ukrainian clock. This silently shifts every time the site generates — event times, invoice dates, automated emails — by two to three hours depending on the season. If booking is added before this is fixed, appointment times will be wrong from day one.

GET /site-properties/v4/properties → "timeZone": "Europe/Kyiv"
High
The live site has no address, phone or opening hours

Every address field is an empty string and there is no phone at all. For a walk-in-radius business in Fitzrovia this is the single biggest local-search handicap there is: Google has nothing to place on a map, and a customer on a phone has nothing to tap.

High
The business email address is bouncing

hello@blanchebeauty.co.uk is flagged as BOUNCED in the live site's own contact record. Anything the site sends to that address — enquiries, notifications, booking alerts — may not be arriving. Worth checking before relying on any email flow.

What needs cleaning up

The 39-service catalogue is complete and mostly well-built. These are the flaws in it.

Medium
All 39 treatments sit in one undifferentiated category

Every service is filed under a single category literally named "Our Services". A customer looking for a brow treatment has to read past manicures, pedicures, lashes and makeup. The natural grouping — Nails, Lashes, Brows, Makeup — is already implicit in the names.

Medium
Deposits are inconsistent, sometimes absurdly so

Men's Manicure is £30 with a £25 deposit — 83% up front. Meanwhile Nail Extensions Infills at £65 takes no deposit at all, and HD Brows at £40 takes none while the nearly identical HD Brows (Shape and Tint) at £40 takes £25. The rule needs to be one rule.

Medium
Near-duplicate services blur the menu

"HD Brows" £40 and "HD Brows (Shape and Tint)" £40 are indistinguishable to a customer. The brow lamination ladder — £50 plain, £60 with tint, £70 with tint and shape — is sensible but needs to read as one choice with options, not three separate products.

Medium
Three services have no duration set

Gel Polish Off & On (£40), Brow Lamination (£50) and Lashes Take Off (£20) carry no session duration. Without it, availability cannot be calculated correctly and the customer cannot see how long to set aside.

Recommended sequence

In dependency order — each step assumes the one before it. Nothing here has been done; all of it waits on your approval.

Fix the live site's business identity

Set the time zone to Europe/London, enter 61 Cleveland Street W1T 4JH, add the phone number, set opening hours. Half an hour of work that unblocks local search and stops every future time being wrong. Also worth confirming the bouncing email separately.

Low risk No data touched Do first

Decide which system owns the calendar — Phorest or Wix

This blocks everything else about booking. Running Phorest alongside Wix Bookings means two calendars that cannot see each other, and double-bookings follow within a week. One system owns availability; the other, at most, links to it.

Blocking decision Needs your call

Migrate the 302 contacts onto the live site

Export from the old site, dedupe against the 10 already on the live one, import. The treatment-history labels are worth carrying across — they are the basis of any future rebooking campaign. Done before anything is retired, never after.

Medium risk Dedupe required Reversible

Stand up booking on the live site

Shape depends on R2. If Phorest owns the calendar, the site embeds or links to Phorest's booking and Wix Bookings is never installed. If Wix owns it, the 39 services are rebuilt on the live site — the catalogue is already fully specified, so this is transcription, not design.

Waits on R2 The revenue step

Clean the catalogue as it is rebuilt

Group into Nails / Lashes / Brows / Makeup, resolve the two HD Brows entries, turn the brow lamination ladder into one service with options, set the three missing durations, and apply one consistent deposit rule. Cheapest to do while rebuilding rather than after.

Low risk Fold into R4

Retire the old site behind a redirect

Only once R3 and R4 are confirmed done. Redirect rather than delete, so the old links and any residual search equity land on the real domain instead of a dead page.

Waits on R3 + R4 Irreversible if deleted

Then, and only then, the design pass

A redesign on top of a site that cannot take a booking is decoration. Once the plumbing works, the design work has something to convert. The brand rules and QA agents for this are already written and committed.

Waits on R4 Needs brand sign-off

What I need from you

Four things block work I would otherwise start now.

Open network access to the site, or check it yourself This environment blocks all outbound web traffic. blanchebeautyuk.com, wixsite.com and phorest.com all return EGRESS_BLOCKED. I could read everything through Wix's API but I could not load a single page — so nothing in this report describes how the site looks, and I could not research Phorest's documentation at all. Allowlisting those domains unblocks both.
Confirm Phorest is definitely the system of record If Blanche's diary already lives in Phorest, that settles R2 and the website's job becomes routing customers into Phorest cleanly. Tell me the plan tier and whether online booking is switched on there, and I will work out the integration options properly.
Which domain is the real one The site runs on blanchebeautyuk.com but every email address is @blanchebeauty.co.uk. I could not test whether the .co.uk domain resolves, redirects, or is unregistered. Customers who type the address off a business card need to land somewhere.
Approval to write to the live site Everything above was read-only. Nothing changes until you approve a specific step — and R1 is safe, fast and worth doing today.

Method

Every figure here was measured on 9 August 2026 through the Wix REST API against the two site IDs, not estimated: site-properties/v4/properties, contacts/v4/contacts, members/v1/members, bookings/v2/services/query and /count, bookings-reader/v2/extended-bookings/count, and events/v3/events/query. All calls were reads. No site content, data or setting was modified. The service catalogue was paged in batches of 13 and deduplicated by service ID, giving 39 of 39. Claims about rendered pages, visual design, SEO tags and Phorest's product capabilities are absent from this report because the network policy made them unverifiable — not because they were checked and found fine.
