CURFEW · TERMS & SAFETY

A helper, not a guarantee

Never a green light to drive. Curfew's blood-alcohol numbers are estimates (Widmark / Watson formulas). Food, medication, health and your own body change the real value substantially. If you can feel the drinks, do not drive — whatever the app shows. The only safe reading for driving is the one from a real breathalyser, and the only truly safe amount is zero.

What Curfew is

Curfew is a self-management tool for pacing alcohol on a night out: counting, water reminders, pacing timers, alarms and a get-home companion. It is not a medical device, not medical advice and not a diagnostic tool. It does not measure anything — it estimates from what you type in.

Safety features are best-effort

Alarms, reminders and the get-home watch depend on your phone: battery, GPS signal, operating-system limits, notification permissions and the app being open. They can fail or arrive late. Never rely on Curfew as your only safety measure. In an emergency call 144 (Switzerland) or 112 (Europe).

If drinking is a struggle

Curfew is made for moderation, not treatment. If alcohol feels bigger than a pacing app, that deserves real support: your doctor, or in Switzerland SafeZone.ch (anonymous, free) and Sucht Schweiz.

Purchase

Curfew is sold as a single one-time unlock through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The purchase, refunds and billing are handled by the store under the store's own terms. No subscription, no recurring charges, no account with us.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, the operator accepts no liability for decisions made on the basis of the app's estimates or for reminders that fail to fire. Using the app does not create any duty of care towards you beyond what the law requires. Swiss law applies.
