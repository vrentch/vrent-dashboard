    About the math

    Blood alcohol is estimated with the Widmark formula, refined by the Watson body-water model using your height and age — and it deliberately takes the more cautious of the two. It assumes a drink is absorbed over ~30 min (empty-ish stomach) and burned off at 0.15‰ per hour. Real life varies a lot; treat the number as a guide, never as permission.
