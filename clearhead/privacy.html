CURFEW · PRIVACY POLICY

You can be honest here.
We can't see any of it.

The short version: Curfew has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and no database of you. What you log on a night out is stored on your phone and nowhere else. We built it this way on purpose — an honesty app only works if nobody is watching.

What stays on your phone (everything)

Your profile (sex, weight, height, age), your drinks and waters, your night history, your reasons ("your why"), your saved home location and your settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are never uploaded, synced or backed up by us. Deleting the app (or using Settings → Erase everything) destroys them permanently — we could not recover them for you even if you asked, because we never had them.

The two narrow exceptions

  • Optional lock-screen alarms (push). If you switch these on, your phone's anonymous push token and the pending alarm times (just timestamps and generic titles like "Water o'clock") are held on our server so the reminder can reach a locked phone. No drinks, no amounts, no location, no identity. Records expire and are deleted automatically within about 28 hours. Switch push off and the schedule is cleared.
  • Typing your home address. If you type an address in "Get home safe", that one address is sent once to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to be turned into map coordinates (geocoding © OpenStreetMap contributors, OSMF privacy policy). The resulting coordinates are stored only on your phone. If you'd rather not, use "use where I am right now" instead — that variant never leaves your device at all. Your live location during the night is read by your phone's GPS locally and is never transmitted anywhere.

Payment

Curfew is a one-time purchase made through the app store (Google Play / Apple App Store). The store processes the payment and knows what stores know; we receive no name, no card details, no address — only the store's anonymous confirmation that this device owns the unlock.

No analytics, no crash reporters, no third-party SDKs

The app contains no advertising or analytics code of any kind. We don't know how often you open it, what you tap, or how much you drink. We prefer it that way.

Your controls

  • Settings → Erase everything wipes profile, nights and history from the device.
  • Disabling lock-screen alarms clears the server-side alarm schedule immediately (and it self-expires within ~28 h regardless).

Legal bits

Because we do not collect personal data, there is essentially nothing for us to hand over, sell, leak or lose. For the two narrow exceptions above, the legal basis is your action requesting them (arming push alarms; looking up an address), and the data is minimised and short-lived as described. This policy is governed by Swiss law (revFADP/DSG); where the GDPR applies to you, the same promises hold.
